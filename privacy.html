Privacy policy

Thyme4Serving for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Last updated 17 November 2026.

In short: Thyme4Serving does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores

Your settings, the foods and cooking steps you add or change, and your saved meals are kept on your own device. The app's built-in food and meal library is part of the app and is not sent anywhere.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app can keep your settings, custom foods and saved meals in step across your own devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. That data is held by Apple under your iCloud account and Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see or access it.

Notifications, alarms and Live Activities

The app asks for permission to show notifications and, on recent versions of iOS, to set alarms, so that cooking reminders still go off when the app is in the background or your device is locked. These are scheduled on your device only. The Live Activity on your Lock Screen shows the next cooking step and is created on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app makes no network requests of its own, uses no third-party code for analytics or advertising, and does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device. To remove synced data from iCloud as well, delete the app from all your devices, or manage iCloud storage in your device's Settings.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
