Getting started

Add the dishes you are cooking, or pick a whole meal. Choose a time to eat, or "as soon as possible", and press Start. The app works out when each dish must go in and rings an alarm for every step, such as turning down the oven, basting or resting the meat.

Common questions

Will alarms go off if my phone is locked?
Yes. Allow notifications (and alarms, if asked) when the app asks. For the loudest, repeating alarm, keep the app open on screen: it can keep the display awake while you cook (Settings).

Can I change a cooking time?
Yes. On the plan, use Adjust times on any step, or edit the food in the library. You can save your change as the new default.

Can I add my own food?
Yes. Use Add a timer or the food library to create your own dish with as many steps as you need.

The times seem wrong for my oven.
Ovens vary. Adjust the steps and save them as your default. Times and temperatures are suggestions only: always check food is cooked through and piping hot before eating, and follow any packaging instructions.

Do my foods and meals sync?
Yes, between your own devices signed in to the same iCloud account.
